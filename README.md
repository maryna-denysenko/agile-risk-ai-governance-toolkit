# Agile, Risk & AI Governance Toolkit

A structured **Docs-as-Code** library of Markdown templates and operational frameworks designed for IT & Agile Project Managers, Scrum Masters, and Product Operations leaders operating in high-stakes, regulated, or AI-enabled environments.

---

## Philosophy: Risk-Resilient Agile Delivery

Most Agile frameworks optimize purely for speed; most legal and compliance checklists optimize purely for risk avoidance. This toolkit bridges both worlds by embedding lightweight, repeatable governance gates directly into Sprint 0 and ongoing delivery rituals.

---
