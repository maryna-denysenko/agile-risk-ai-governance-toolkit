# Pre-Launch IP & Trademark Compliance Gate

> **Purpose:** Eliminate intellectual property, trademark, copyright, and platform policy risks *before* product assets, designs, or marketing listings go live across US, UK, and EU jurisdictions. Designed for e-commerce, digital products, and high-velocity marketplaces where a single violation triggers listing takedowns or permanent account suspension.

## 2. The 4-Point Pre-Launch Compliance Gate

All four gates must pass before moving an asset or listing to production.

### Gate 1 — Trademark Screening (TM)
- [ ] Screened product titles, brand names, phrases, and slogans against official registries (**USPTO**, **EUIPO**, **UKIPO**) for target Nice classes
- [ ] Checked phonetic equivalents, plural forms, and common spelling variations for likelihood of confusion
- [ ] Verified zero unauthorized references to protected franchises, celebrities, sports teams, or registered trade dress

### Gate 2 — Copyright & Commercial Licensing Audit
- [ ] Confirmed graphic artwork, illustrations, and visual layouts are 100% original or backed by documented commercial-use licenses
- [ ] Verified typography / font licenses explicitly permit commercial distribution and digital embedding
- [ ] Audited third-party stock assets and AI-assisted visual elements for clear commercial rights and policy compliance

### Gate 3 — SEO & Metadata Keyword Quarantine
- [ ] Separated competitor brand names, third-party trademarks, and risky terms into a dedicated **Excluded / Quarantine Tab** during keyword mining[cite: 3]
- [ ] Audited front-end copy (**Title, Bullets, Description, URL slug**) for zero accidental brand or trademark inclusion[cite: 4]
- [ ] Audited hidden **Back-End Search Terms** and metadata attributes to confirm strict exclusion of third-party marks[cite: 4]
- [ ] Applied trademarked competitor terms to **PPC Negative Keyword** lists where required by channel policy

### Gate 4 — Platform Style Guides & Policy Verification
- [ ] Reviewed listing formatting, claims, and imagery against current marketplace/channel Style Guides[cite: 4]
- [ ] Completed final language QA / proofreading to eliminate ambiguous claims or policy-triggering typos[cite: 4]
- [ ] Confirmed post-launch indexing and live compliance check is scheduled within 48 hours of publication[cite: 4]
